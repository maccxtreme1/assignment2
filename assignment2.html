FOOD NAME

Ofe Akwu and Rice


IMAGE


DESCRIPTION

It is a mixture of akwu soup and rice


INGREDIENTS

  • Palm fruit concentrate
  • Beef,goat or assorted meat
  • Stockfish
  • Dried or smoked fish
  • Crayfish
  • Fresh pepper or ground pepper
  • Onions
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Salt
  • Water or meat stock


COOKING STEPS

  1. Wash and season the meat
  2. Cook the meat until tender
  3. Prepare the banga soup
  4. Boil the banga banga soup for 15 minutes
  5. Add the meat and stock
  6. Add pepper,crayfish and spices
  7. Add fish and cook for 10 minutes
  8. Add the soup leaves
  9. Simmer for 5 minutes
  10. Serve hot with your preffered swallow


COOKING TIME

It takes 50 minutes
